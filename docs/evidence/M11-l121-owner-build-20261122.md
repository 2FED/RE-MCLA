- The shm-hot ON arms keep their bodies; with this SDK they mean the fixed lever. `pc/shm-hot-span` is the
  l120 lever, only for reproducing KI-090.
- Built and unit-tested only: not signed, not run, no physical PASS.
- **Spark not used**: these are deterministic source edits, builds, scripted generation and checks; no
  bounded extraction or triage task suited it.

## 6. What the owner runs decide

- **Run 1 (verify).**
  - The five must-be-0 counters must be 0, with no BAD line. A non-zero value stops the ON runs, and its
    BAD lines name the page.
  - `cmp_page_diff_span_same` above 0 confirms the precondition of KI-090 candidate 1: writes outside the
    requested bytes of hot pages, which the l120 lever left stale and l121 copies.
  - `MCLA_SHM_HOT_PAGES` shows which consumers (vertex slot, index) the hot pages belong to.
  - The cost: `cmp_us_per` and `cmp_ms` (whole page plus span in verify) and `emul` (the emulated faults
    the ON mode would make).
- **Run 2 (ON).**
  - No white square in 20+ minutes with races, and nothing else seen.
  - In every line `global_fire_cleared=0` and `gpu_exit_after_mark=0`.
  - fps and CP against the clear run.
- **Run 3 (span, optional).** The square coming back with the l120 lever, and not with the l121 lever,
  would point to F1 or F3 as the fix. Span mode keeps F2, so the A/B does not separate F1 from F3.

## 7. Risks

- Nothing ran in the game. KI-090's cause is not proven: l121 removes every candidate the analysis named, and
  the verify measures each of them.
- The ON mode now makes the same over-invalidations as the old path, so its saving is smaller than l120's.
  The emulated faults fire watches on the CP thread inside vertex and index requests (SDK document,
  section 7).
- A same-bytes rewrite next to texture data is still a difference from the old path (`tex_watch_missed`).
- Hot requests take the global lock on the CP thread. The whole-page compare is about 32 ns per page on
  the PC; under Wine it is extrapolated.
- KI-085: the new arm issues no GPU queries, so it is safe in races.
