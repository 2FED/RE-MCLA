## Method

As in `M10-001-run1-1440p-cold-20261101.md` / `M10-001-720p-and-ki047-20261101.md`: segments are
the sums of the `MCLA_PERF_SPLIT` windows between consecutive F10 windows (stationary = start F10
to the 90 s F10; laps between the next F10s); every per-frame value is segment sum / segment
frames. CP work = `cp_busy - fence_wait`; render work = `render_cpu - render_wait` (spin in
sub_82411E98). `MCLA_THREAD_CPU` gives per-thread CPU. New counters (`MCLA_WATCH`, `MCLA_READBACK`,
`MCLA_READBACK_SYNC`, `MCLA_WRM`) are windowed per perf-split window, **but their `kind=minute`
lines cover the whole minute and are not reset by an F10 `kind=mark`** (checked: `frames=`/`secs=`
and `resolves=` against the perf split). Each minute line was therefore reduced by the marks since
the previous minute line before summing; after that correction `MCLA_WRM wait_ms` equals the perf
split `wrm_mem_ms` and `MCLA_READBACK_SYNC resolves` equals `copies` window by window.
`MCLA_REPLAY_WAKE`, `watch protect/min`, frame pacing and gpu busy are per minute only.

Pauses inside segments (guest paused, vblank back to 60 Hz) were kept: 1440 base#1 lap 2 5.3 s,
1440 all#1 lap 1 4.9 s, 720 all lap 1 14.7 s (5.8 + 8.0 + 0.9 s, 10% of the lap). Lap lengths
differ between sessions (base#2 lap 1 190 s, all#1 lap 2 146 s, 720 all lap 2 165 s against
90-112 s), so the stationary segment is the cleanest A/B; laps are indicative.

## 2. The 720p regression (l53 56-62 fps -> l63 base 43-46 fps): decomposition

Frame interval = CP busy + wrm_mem + ring idle in every segment (the CP is the serial chain in
l63; in l53 it was the render thread with the CP 1-3 ms behind it).

| ms per frame | l53 A stat | l63 base stat | delta | l53 A lap 2 | l53 B lap 2 | l63 base lap 2 | delta vs A / B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interval | 16.35 | 23.20 | **+6.85** | 17.78 | 16.57 | 21.93 | **+4.15 / +5.36** |
| Draws/frame | 6652 | 7417 (+11.5%) | | 6526 | 6113 | 6237 | (-4% / +2%) |
| KI-047 forced wait (fence_wait) | 0.00 | **5.03** | **+5.03** | 0.05 | 0.04 | **4.60** | **+4.55 / +4.56** |
| CP work (busy - fence) | 13.58 | 16.22 | +2.64 | 15.34 | 14.05 | 15.44 | +0.10 / +1.39 |
| wrm_mem | 1.93 | 1.93 | 0 | 1.93 | 1.94 | 1.87 | -0.06 |
| ring idle | 0.84 | 0.02 | -0.82 | 0.46 | 0.54 | 0.02 | -0.44 / -0.52 |
| GPU busy ms/f (% wall) | 12.57 (77%) | 13.46 (58%) | | 12.99 (73%) | 12.40 (75%) | 13.17 (60%) | |
| Render work ms/f | 15.91 | 18.17 | +2.26 | 16.51 | 15.47 | 16.19 | -0.3 / +0.7 |
| Render spin share | 0.6% | 20.6% | | 4.5% | 4.3% | 24.7% | |

`MCLA_READBACK_SYNC` (base, 720p): 1.02-1.21 forced composite waits per frame (rule c 98%),
**4.7-5.1 ms of wait per frame = 21-22% of wall**, 3.9-5.0 ms per wait (a full GPU drain:
the GPU is ~5 ms of work behind the CP at the composite resolve). `forced_wait_failed=0`.

The stationary +2.64 ms of CP work is fully itemised by content: draw ms 7.26 -> 8.84 (+1.58:
+0.84 from 11.5% more draws at l53 per-draw cost, +0.74 from 0.10 us/draw more, mostly `p_tex`
0.229 -> 0.292, a scene effect), upload +0.51, copy outside the read-back wait +0.16, packets
other +0.26, submit/execute/present +0.08 (sum 2.63). On lap 2, where the draw count is
comparable, CP work is unchanged against l53 A (15.44 vs 15.34) and the whole delta is the
forced wait.

Other candidates, measured:

- **Census (`memory_watch_census=1`)**: `watch protect/min` per-call cost l63 base 720p arm
  0.64-0.71 us, disarm 0.50-0.60 us; l53 720p arm 0.62-0.69 us, disarm 0.45-0.58 us. Lock wait
  46-66 ms/min (l63) vs 55-90 ms/min (l53). At ~1100 arm and ~430 disarm calls per frame the
  census adds at most ~0.05-0.1 ms/frame. The census's own `MCLA_WATCH` hold times (arm hold
  1.46-1.49 ms/f, disarm hold 0.51-0.55 ms/f) are the protect-path times with the census
  bookkeeping inside; they cannot be compared to l53 (no census build). Upper bound for census
  + livery diag v3 + content per draw: CP work per 1000 draws is 2.19/2.39/2.48 ms (l63 base)
  vs 2.04/2.31/2.35 (l53 A) = +3.5-7.4% per draw, i.e. <= 0.6-1.1 ms/f including the scene
  effect (p_tex), against 4.6-5.0 ms/f of forced wait. **Not the culprit.**
- **Livery diag v3 lines**: ~5,400-6,000 `MCLA_LIVERY_DIAG2` lines (2.4 MB) per 9-minute
  session (~10 lines/s, rate limited); l53 had diag off in these sessions. CPU cost of the
  bookkeeping (tex_loads_tracked ~135k/min) is not separately measurable here; bounded by the
  same <= 0.6-1.1 ms/f residual. **Not the culprit.**
- **Perf split mode**: mode 1 (1 in 16) in both builds; unsampled-check ratio 0.78-0.80 in
  l63 base vs 1.20-1.29 in l53 (the ratio is distorted by the long fence wait inside the draw
  packet scope; not a cost).
- **Route**: BENCH-A stationary is a heavier scene (+11.5% draws, higher p_tex). Without the
  forced wait, l63 base would run at CP chain ~18.2 ms / render work 18.2 ms = ~55 fps
  stationary and ~56 fps on lap 2 (l53 A lap 2: 56.2).

**Culprit: the KI-047 full-drain read-back sync** (composite rule, about one forced wait per
frame). It converts the CP into a serial CP + GPU-latency chain: 4.6-5.0 ms/frame at 720p
(73% of the stationary drop, ~100% of the lap 2 drop), 14-15.5 ms/frame at 1440p. The GPU
then idles (busy 58-60% at 720p, 82-86% at 1440p). The remaining ~2.6 ms stationary is scene
content (BENCH-A vs BENCH-B), not a build regression.

## 6. Recommendations

Next volunteer build:

1. **Turn the targeted read-back sync on by default** (with the KI-047 composite fix). Net win
   at both resolutions (forced wait -3.4 ms/f at 720p, -6 to -7 ms/f at 1440p), zero fallbacks
   and zero failed waits in ~25 minutes of driving. Gate: owner visual check of the police
   livery in a targeted-only build (the KI-047 counters stay at 0 but the visual is the
   acceptance).
2. **Turn read-back elision on**: -3 ms/f of GPU work, CPU bytes and guard counters identical.
   Fix the `sporadic_read_prev_unfenced` diagnostic for never-written slots first (or note it).
3. **Native refcount, WRM event wake, replay batched wake**: include (render-thread sub_82422160
   to 0%, ~0.8 ms/f off the CP chain, ~0.4 core freed); low risk on these counters.
4. **Drop upload-ahead 16 and disarm bridging from the PC default**: both are net negative here
   (+20 MB/f uploads, +60% render write faults, slower disarms). Keep them as Deck-only
   experiments, where the arm/disarm syscall cost profile differs.
5. **Census off** in volunteer builds (cheap, but no use there); livery diag v3 can stay in
   owner builds only.
6. The volunteer l56 build carries the full drain: expect ~-22% at 720p, ~-25-30% at 1080p
   (estimate), ~-15% at 1440p against an unsynchronised build; ship the targeted wait (+elision)
   to volunteers soon.

Single-arm tests still needed (BENCH-A, same stationary spot, 1440p and 720p; 1080p once):

- base + targeted only (no elision) and base + elision only: separates the two biggest wins.
- targeted with kicks off (or kick_draws 1024/2048): kicks triple the submissions at 720p and
  raise per-draw CP cost; check whether a larger interval keeps the wait gain.
- all minus upload-ahead and bridging: the expected volunteer configuration; predicts ~52-55 fps
  stationary at 720p if the ~2 ms of extra CP work goes away.
- no-sync control (`mcla_readback_sync_composite=false`) on BENCH-A for the true ceiling of this
  route (the 55 fps estimate above).
- 1080p base vs targeted+elide to replace the interpolated volunteer cost.
