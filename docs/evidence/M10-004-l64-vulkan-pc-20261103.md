### Vulkan is pinned at the stock 30 fps cap, not GPU-limited

- The Vulkan session logs "MCLA guest vblank: 60 Hz" once and never switches; the D3D12 sessions switch to
  "MCLA guest vblank: 240 Hz" about 45 s after start. Vblank counter rates confirm it (60.0/s vs 240/s).
- Cause (SDK read-only, graphics_system.cpp): the vblank thread forces the override to 0 while
  `g_mcla_ui_capped` is true. `g_mcla_ui_capped` is initialised to true and only cleared by
  `MclaReportGuestFrameDraws()`, whose only caller is `d3d12/command_processor.cpp:5003`. The Vulkan
  command processor never reports frame draws, so the UI cap never releases and Unlocked never engages
  under Vulkan. (The menu-state module still toggles mcla_ui_cap_draws_low 250/100000 in the log, which
  has no effect because the flag itself is never cleared.)
- Headroom evidence on the 5060: frame_fence_wait 0.02 ms/frame (iGPU 0.55-1.33), swap packet 2.4-2.7 ms
  (iGPU 13-14), command_wait_reg_mem 8.5-10.5 ms/frame waiting for the guest, async_placeholder_skips
  only 19 in total (log warning "Skipping Vulkan frame presentation..." printed once). The GPU is mostly
  idle; the frame rate is set by the 60 Hz vblank (game runs at half the vblank).
- Per-frame Vulkan CP costs (dGPU): draw packets ~12.6-13.9 ms, binding updates ~4.2-5.0 ms, descriptor
  updates ~1.9-2.2 ms, deferred execute ~4.4-4.9 ms, non-MMIO write faults ~1.6 ms, ~6200-7400 draws/frame.
- The iGPU Vulkan run was 19-20 fps by the counters (owner perceived 10-14 fps, likely the hitches).

## 2. The glitch at the low centre of the screen

Frames compared (full-res crops of x 320-960, y 400-720 and 3x zooms): Vulkan packages 1 and 4 against
l63 base and all package 1 (same start mark as Vulkan package 4), plus the iGPU Vulkan package 2.

- It sits on the player car, which the chase camera keeps at the low centre. Package 1: a soft, translucent,
  vertical light smear (a blurred pole with a bright spot) running from the bottom of the rear window
  across the boot lid through the badge. Package 4: a soft horizontal oval smear on the boot lid just below
  the Chrysler badge. Both are confined to the body/glass, consistent with the owner's "lines that turn into
  an oval when the car turns".
- Same mark under D3D12: the car shows the camo livery, and the bumper/boot carry a sharp reflection of the
  city skyline (environment reflection). Under Vulkan (dGPU and iGPU alike) the livery is missing (car
  renders black), the skyline reflection is absent, and instead the edges carry coloured glints (red, and on
  the iGPU also green/yellow) plus the smear. So the paint layers of the car shader are wrong, not a screen
  post-process.
- Classification: player car's own reflection (environment-reflection texture sampled wrongly / with stale
  or wrong-content render-to-texture), not bloom/glare/lens flare/sun occlusion/velocity blur and not a UI
  element. The reflection direction changes as the car yaws, which explains the shape change. Confidence
  medium (from frames only; no GPU capture).
- Livery missing under Vulkan on both GPUs is a separate, clear finding. It contradicts the KI-062 note
  "police livery looked correct"; the player's camo livery is absent in every Vulkan frame. Likely the
  KI-047 mechanism: Vulkan runs readback_resolve "some" (217 readback copies, 1.9 s total wait by package 3)
  but has none of the MCLA D3D12 fixes (d3d12_readback_sync_composite, the KI-047 composite sync; the
  Vulkan source has zero MCLA patches), so the livery composite read back for the CPU is stale or empty.
- The environment reflection may share the cause (a render-to-texture result consumed through the
  resolve/readback path before it is current) or be a Vulkan resolve/format issue; not proven.
- Device creation: no feature/extension failure. Extensions: swapchain, custom_border_color,
  fragment_shader_interlock, memory_budget, non_seamless_cube_map, robustness2. Only format fallbacks are the
  two packed YUV 4:2:2 formats (k_Cr_Y1_Cb_Y0_REP, k_Y1_Cr_Y0_Cb_REP -> VK format 37), video only. 559 of
  559 pipelines loaded from storage. Implicit layers present: NV optimus/present and OBS hook.
- See-through wheel rims and road artifacts: not identifiable in these five frames (rims hidden from the
  chase view, road at frame resolution looks normal). Jordan billboard renders darker red than under D3D12
  (possibly time of day; not claimed).

## 3. Verdict and Deck prediction

- PC: no speed verdict is possible from this run. Vulkan on the 5060 holds a flat 29.8-29.9 fps because
  Unlocked never engages under Vulkan (missing MclaReportGuestFrameDraws in the Vulkan command processor);
  D3D12 Unlocked on the same GPU and loop does 43-51 fps. At the 30 fps cap Vulkan shows ample GPU headroom
  (fence wait ~0). Vulkan also renders wrongly (livery, car reflection), so D3D12 stays the PC backend.
- Deck: a Vulkan Unlocked arm will also be capped at the stock 30 fps rate (same code path), so any Deck
  "Vulkan Unlocked vs D3D12 Unlocked" comparison would be invalid until the Vulkan CP reports frame draws
  (or the UI cap is bypassed when the backend is not D3D12). The iGPU run (20 fps, under the cap) is the
  only real Vulkan throughput datum; it suggests the Deck APU on native Vulkan would be GPU/CP-bound below 30
  at 720p. The livery and car-reflection glitches are backend logic, not vendor-specific, so expect them on
  the Deck too.
- Next: wire MclaReportGuestFrameDraws into the Vulkan command processor (per guest frame draw count), port
  the KI-047 composite sync to Vulkan, then repeat the l64 loop for a real Vulkan vs D3D12 fps comparison.
