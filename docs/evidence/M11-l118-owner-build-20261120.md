## 6. Risks

- Nothing ran in l118. The vertex-slot fix changes the default path: a refused skip costs one
  RequestRange (as upstream); the check itself is estimated at 0.05-0.2 ms of CP per frame (not
  measured). If MCLA_VB shows `_invalid` at 0 in the owner runs, the defect was latent for this
  game and the fix only costs the check.
- The 1x scene loses MSAA (aliasing; render state 15 off as in the game's 1x path; the collector
  depth at 1x); the FXAA arm is the comparison.
- rt_memo2 must follow a clean rt-memo2-verify; the verify checks only draws the memo would skip.
- The Vulkan edit of `8d351cc` is not compiled in our builds.
- KI-085: the heavy GPU verify arms stay out of races.
