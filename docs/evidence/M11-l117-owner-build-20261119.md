## 6. Risks

- Nothing ran in l117. The levers' hit rates and savings are estimates from Phase A (0.3-0.7 ms of
  CP per frame at 6 k draws together; see the SDK document, section 3).
- cp-fast2 must follow a clean cp-verify2. The verifies check only the draws where the lever would
  have reused something; a missed invalidation outside those would not show there.
- L2 (index buffer view cache) is negligible on the CP thread (one deferred-list append per skipped
  set); it is kept because it is exact and cheap.
- KI-085: the heavy GPU verify arms stay out of races; cp-verify2 is not one of them.
