## 2. What is wrong in the frames

- **Mesh and decals are fine.** The car is the full-detail mesh (lightbar,
  antennas, push bar, Chevrolet badge, readable plate). The door decals (city
  seal, "POLICE", "19350", the motto text) are crisp and in place at both scales.
  This is not LOD, mip or anisotropy blur.
- **The body paint/livery layer contains foreign, tile-structured content.**
  - 2160p (`42608-1`): the black/white two-tone split is mostly right, but the
    white doors and roof carry rows of small black vertical tick marks (paired
    "H"-like marks in a regular grid) and smudge clusters near the seal. The rear
    (`42608-2`) is mostly normal black.
  - 1440p (`29760-1/-2/-3`): much worse. Doors, rear quarter, trunk side and the
    front fender are covered with vertical, blocky streaks of **blue**, black,
    grey, white and some red. Blue is not part of the LAPD livery. The streaks run
    in columns, like a blocky texture pasted over the panels. They are still there
    in gameplay at 20 m (`-3`).
  - The red areas on the hood and fenders in both sessions have hard edges and
    match the lit red lightbar. They are probably the light projection, not part
    of the defect (low confidence).
- **Same defect class at both scales, different content.** It is identical in
  kind (foreign blocky content in the paint layer, decals intact) but not
  identical in pattern (sparse ticks at scale 3, dense blue streaks at scale 2).
  That fits "wrong source data", whose content depends on what else was
  composited or resolved earlier. It does not fit a fixed decode error.
- **Not the KI-038/032 band pattern.** KI-038 shows horizontal red/yellow/blue
  bands, one shared image that replaces the whole sign. Here the decals survive
  and the corruption is vertical, tile-like and scale-dependent. Today's frames
  show normal signs (WILCOX, HIGHLAND, REED, the Zaxby's billboard), so KI-038 did
  not appear in this run.
- **Not the 2026-10-29 "flat white low-LOD body".** That earlier observation was
  distant, blocky low-LOD meshes with uniform white paint. Today's cars are
  full-LOD with patterned corruption. The earlier frame was probably ordinary
  distance LOD, or a different symptom.

## 4. Mechanism and ranked hypotheses

Known pipeline (KI-033, M8 evidence): the game composites a car's paint/vinyl on
the GPU into a 32bpp target (the editor shows 2048 RGBA8), **resolves it, reads the
resolve output on the CPU**, and the car is then drawn from a 1024 BC3 atlas.
That is why the KI-033 coarseness was fixed in the scaled-readback downscale
shader (`678adc4`, 32bpp layout only). With draw scale > 1, the resolve writes
only the scaled buffer. The CPU sees it only through
`D3D12CommandProcessor::IssueCopy_ReadbackResolvePath`.

1. **Shifted scaled-readback CPU copy (scale > 1 only). Rank 1, medium.**
   `render_target_cache.cpp` makes the scaled range current from `copy_dest_base`
   but reports `written_address = copy_dest_extent_start`. The downscale reads from
   `GetCurrentScaledResolveRangeStartScaled()` (the base) and `memcpy`s to
   `written_address`. Any resolve whose extent starts after the base therefore gets
   the wrong region's data. A vinyl/livery atlas resolved slot by slot or row by row
   would copy another slot (another car's vinyl, which explains the blue) into the
   police car's slot. The content would depend on what the atlas held (a different
   pattern per session/scale) while separate decal textures stay correct. It
   explains "both 1440p and 2160p". It predicts that 720p (scale 1, where the
   unscaled branch copies from `written_address`) is clean. KI-033's fixture
   explicitly did not validate extent/origin handling.
2. **Delayed and sparse readback staleness (any scale). Rank 2, medium-low.**
   `readback_resolve=some` copies to guest memory the *previous* resolve with the
   same (address, length) key. It copies only on a cache miss or every 4th resolve
   (`kReadbackSomeRefreshInterval`), and does not wait on a fence for the read slot.
   A livery composited once per spawned car into a reused scratch surface would get
   an older car's contents. This also explains foreign content, but it predicts that
   720p is broken too. It has been the default since l20 (`fast`, also delayed,
   before that). The owner's garage vinyl checks (own car, repeatedly resolved)
   would not expose it.
3. **Downscale layout for non-32bpp formats (scale > 1). Rank 3, low.** The 64bpp
   downscale still uses the pre-fix naive layout (untested). 128bpp and other sizes
   return silently with no CPU copy. The livery target is 32bpp according to KI-033,
   so this is unlikely to be the main cause.
4. **Texture-cache eviction under the DXGI budget (KI-048). Rank 4, very low.**
   The 1440p session had no pressure and is the worse one. Eviction reloads the
   same source; it cannot produce foreign blue content.
5. **LOD, mip or anisotropy. Excluded for these frames.** Full mesh, crisp decals;
   the corruption is content, not blur.
6. **Genuine game livery. Excluded.** Blue streaks are not LAPD paint, and the
   pattern differs between sessions.

Has the owner ever seen this car correct at 720p (scale 1)? **No recorded
observation** in known-issues, evidence or the current-state handoff (KI-047 was
first reported at 2160p on l41). Ask the owner. A "yes, 720p always looked right"
answer would itself favour H1.

Is it the same path as the M8 "vinyl packing" fix (`678adc4`)? It is the **same
CPU readback-of-resolve path** (the scaled branch of
`IssueCopy_ReadbackResolvePath`), but a different defect. `678adc4` fixed the
per-pixel sample layout for 32bpp. H1 is the range origin (base versus extent
start), which that fix did not touch.

## 6. Relation to KI-038/032

Probably **not the same mechanism**. KI-038/032 are a single shared band image
replacing whole signs, also seen at 720p, and intermittent at 4K. KI-047 is
foreign, scale-dependent content in a CPU-consumed livery, with decals intact.
They share the scaled readback path as a suspect (H2 of the KI-038 analysis is H1
here). The same shifted-copy fix is the common candidate, so an H1 confirmation
here would raise its priority for KI-038/032. It would not prove those issues
fixed.

## Unresolved risks

- The vinyl/livery atlas layout (whether slots are resolved with an extent offset
  from the destination base) is inferred, not traced. A one-session log of scaled
  readbacks with `written_address != copy_dest_base` would confirm it directly.
- The scale-3 frame looks less damaged than the scale-2 one. That is consistent
  with content dependence but not explained in detail.
- No owner text in report.txt. The defect description comes from the frames only.
