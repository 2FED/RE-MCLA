## Verdict per the decision rule

- **B is within 10% of A at matched draws at both resolutions**: 2160p within 1.5% fps and
  1.6% GPU time per frame; 1440p within about 2-5% fps (derived tail) and 0.9% GPU time per
  frame (W1). l33-sunc is not faster than l41; if anything it is marginally slower at 2160p.
- **GPU is the limiter in both at 2160p**: 59.5 / 59.6 s busy per minute. At 1440p the
  sessions are too short to show a GPU-busy minute after the vblank switch; W1 is held at
  30.8 fps by the 60 Hz guest vblank (GPU 53% busy) for both builds alike.
- **2160p VRAM pressure is present on both builds, identically**: pre-existing, not l41.
  1440p has no pressure on either.
- Per the rule, KI-046 closes as "no regression; GPU-bound"; the 2160p VRAM over-budget
  becomes a separate S2 item (pre-existing, affects l33-sunc as well).

## Anomalies and limits

- The sessions ran 18:13-18:23 local time (16:13-16:23 UTC), not after 20:00.
- No session reached 3 minutes: 1440p 1:55 / 1:59, 2160p 2:09 / 2:07. 1440p therefore has
  no clean per-minute window; its comparison rests on cumulative exit counters (fps only)
  and on W1 GPU cost. A repeat 1440p pair with at least 3 minutes would give a GPU-busy
  minute, but it is unlikely to change the verdict: the two builds produced near-identical
  counters (same draw percentiles, replay volume, watch stats) wherever both were measured.
- B 2160p created 625 pipelines from storage in 1568 ms (A: 1073 in 174 ms), so l33's
  scale-3 cache was less warm; it may explain B's slightly higher >50 ms count (486 vs 427).
- All four receipts match the intended build, mode (unlocked) and target; nothing was
  mislabelled.
