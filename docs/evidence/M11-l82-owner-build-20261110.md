## 1. Purpose

Deck lever 2 A/B: CP bulk register writes. The SDK cvar `cp_bulk_register_writes` (default false) copies runs of plain register writes in one go instead of one virtual `WriteRegister` per dword (docs/evidence/M11-l82-sdk-bulk-registers-20261108.md). The owner key `cp_bulk_regs=true` switches it on. The A/B compares `packets_other`, `regs_bulk`, `regs_single`, `bulk_runs` (MCLA_PERF_SPLIT), CP busy and fps on `default` vs `bulkregs`, mainly on the Steam Deck (estimate, not measured: Deck -1 to -2.5 ms/f of 10.8 ms/f `packets_other`; PC -0.3 to -0.8 ms/f). No visual difference is expected.
