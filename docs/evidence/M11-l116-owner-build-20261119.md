## 1. Host change

- **Plumbing** (follows the l99 hitch / VRAM entry pattern: resolved by name from rexruntime.dll, a
  no-op against an older SDK):
  - `src/mcla_low_latency.h` / `src/mcla_low_latency_win.cpp`: `MclaSdkPlayWindowFocus`,
    `MclaSdkPlayGamePaused`. They resolve `rex_play_window_focus` and `rex_play_game_paused`

    `MCLA_PLAY_HOST v=1 kind=entry sdk_entry=found|absent`.

    the existing `MCLA_WINDOW_EVENT` line).

    racer_off)` is published at every change. It is the flag that forces the UI cap and is logged as
    `MCLA menu state: game paused|running`, and it is computed in the Locked mode too. It is not
    published after `ShutdownMenuStateCap`.

## 6. Risks

- Nothing ran in l116. The skip relies on the focus and pause events reaching the SDK. The proof
  line is `MCLA_PLAY_HOST ... sdk_entry=found` followed by `guard_skip` lines during the alt-tab; if
  they are missing, the guard sees only the outlier rule.
- The guard default changed from latch to cool-down. A real lead-2 burst in play now costs 60 s of
  strict and then Reflex again. If flicker returns after a re-arm, the backoff limits it to 4 quick
  trips before the session latch.
- MCLA_BLACK may have no data in normal play if the front buffer is not on the CP read-back delivery
  path (`black_checked=0` on the minute lines). No new read-back was added.
- KI-085: the heavy verify arms stay out of races.
